# Arrive AI

> Arrive AI (Nasdaq: ARAI) is an autonomous last-mile delivery company headquartered in
> Fishers, Indiana. Its patented Autonomous Last Mile (ALM) platform is anchored by the
> Arrive Point, an AI-powered smart mailbox that accepts and dispatches parcels to and
> from drones, ground robots and human couriers, with temperature-controlled
> compartments, real-time tracking, smart logistics alerts and chain-of-custody controls.

Founded by Dan O'Toole (originally as DroneDek), Arrive AI began direct trading on the
Nasdaq Stock Market as ARAI on 2025-05-15. It sells hardware plus a network service into
healthcare, pharmacy, retail and general parcel logistics.
